<p>Originally place for a gamekeeper, now a special spot known as the Vango Wonderland. With any luck you might encounter a hare, a fox, a lynx or perhaps a hobbit. They say even mermaids have been spotted. Here you can wander the forests and open your ears and heart to hear the voices of the trees. You might also want to spend your time going to sauna, swimming, having a picnic or playing volleyball. Some take time here to celebrate a birthday, some to organize a seminar. Some choose to take a few gracious yoga poses to greet the sun, others prefer sunbathing in a boat on a little lake and sing along to birds. Whatever brings you to Vango, we are eager to hear from you.</p><p class="fact">Sleeps: approx. 55+ beds</p>
