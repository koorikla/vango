<p>is a place where every journeyer ends up at some point. It’s a place for relaxation or having a meal in front of the fireplace. Before taking up a new journey, this sauna should be paid a visit to. They say the journey will be more beautiful and road to destination shorter if you visit Journeyer’s sauna beforehand.</p><p class="fact">Sleeps: 11 beds on the second floor, and sofa beds downstairs.</p><p>Additional info: hot tub on the sauna terrace is a perfect place to admire the starry sky.</p>
