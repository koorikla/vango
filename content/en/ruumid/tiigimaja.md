<p>lies on the side of the little lake and gazes up to the moon. This romantic dwelling is full of cute chambers and its heart is the luminous Moon Room. Moon Room is known for being difficult to leave. So strong can be the pull of the moon.</p><p class="fact">Sleeps: 15 ppl</p><p>Additional info: Old renovated log house that has a three and four-bed room upstairs and two-bed rooms downstairs. Events for approx. 20 ppl can be organized in the Moon Room.</p>
