In July 2018 the Võnge festival came to the Wonderland. Two days of music among
the trees — the largest event held here so far.

Võnge was never a big festival in the ordinary sense. Small stages, acoustic
sets, concerts in daylight, and rather more lying in the grass than dancing
through the night. Less a festival than a walk into nature that happens to have
a ticket. That is exactly why it fitted here: the Garden of Sounds holds
hundreds of people at once, and the forest around it still keeps the whole
thing intimate.

On 7 July, Jaan Pehk played the Valmiermuiža stage.
