In October, young people from the Tahkuranna Open Youth Centre came to the
Wonderland for two days of fish school — autumn study days about catching fish
and making the most of what you catch.

Adventure-club instructor Norman Talson led the whole way from landing a fish
to cooking it. To keep minds awake as well as hands, the group spent time
painting the nature around them, guided by Ave Lepik. Eli Sukles came along as
trainer.

The project behind it, “Games for the parish through cooperation”, nearly fell
through at the last moment because of the pandemic restrictions — but the days
happened anyway. The same young people had already spent a longer camp here
during the summer, so the Wonderland was familiar ground.

By the end everyone wanted the same thing: to learn something new together
again.
