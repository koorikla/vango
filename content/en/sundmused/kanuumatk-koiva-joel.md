On the Monday before midsummer there was room to join
[Livonia Matkad](http://www.livoniamatkad.ee/) on a canoe trip down the Gauja,
the river Estonians call the Koiva.

Spring showers had watered northern Latvia thoroughly, so the river's sandy
beaches were narrower than usual — but the water was already around 18 °C, and
a couple of dry days would have brought the beaches back. Nineteen kilometres
of paddling.

An experienced guide picked the group up by bus from the Wonderland yard, took
them to the river, and drove them straight back to a hot sauna afterwards. That
is the shape of the day: the river in the morning, steam in the evening.

Canoe trips are still one of the simplest ways to give a whole day at Vango over
to the outdoors. Ask us at [info@vango.ee](mailto:info@vango.ee), or go straight
to Livonia Matkad.
