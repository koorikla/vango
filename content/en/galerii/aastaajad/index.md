translationKey: "galerii-aastaajad"

summary: "One place, four faces — pictures through the year."
---

Vango looks different in every season, and that is one reason people keep
coming back.

In spring the apple tree beside the Garner of the Smart Oak turns pink. In
summer the little lake is warm and Aladdin's Lea is full of tents. In autumn
the forest goes quiet and the baskets fill with boletus. By November there is
only mist, black water, and a fire showing from a long way off.
