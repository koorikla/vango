Since the spring of 2020 the Wonderland has had a private Kärg — “honeycomb” —
an inventive little house built from straw board and entirely natural
materials, which takes its energy directly from the sun. It can even be moved
if need be.

Kärg is at its best in cooler weather, when you can curl up inside, watch the
stars and more or less hear the mushrooms growing. People who have stayed there
describe it as innovative right down to its foundations: the house runs on
solar power, and the company's slogan is simply “Off The Grid Cabin”.

More about the cabin itself at [karg.ee](https://www.karg.ee).
