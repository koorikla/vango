When a spring evening starts to dim, steam rises off the hot tub like a small
white spell.

Warm water, cool evening air, laughter and long conversations all run together
here — along with that particular feeling that the world can wait a while. Just
sit, listen to the forest, look up, and let the spring warm you back through.

Sometimes happiness needs nothing more than good company, hot water, and a
little wonderland around it.

The tub sits on the terrace of the Journeyer's Sauna, under an open sky. It is
waiting for anyone who wants to step out of an ordinary week and into a folk
tale for a night.
