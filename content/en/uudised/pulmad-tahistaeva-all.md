Every couple's own wedding is the most special one — and yet now and then even
well-travelled guests are surprised.

The summer of 2020 brought a wedding to the Wonderland whose colour was deep
blue and whose room was hung full of stars. The ceremony began only once
darkness had fallen: after sunset, by starlight, moonlight and candlelight. The
rings were exchanged in the Vango yard, late in the evening.

What could be more romantic than exchanging rings and dancing until morning
under a sky like that?

The Wonderland was the venue,
[Zefiir Peokorraldus](https://www.facebook.com/zefiirpeokorraldus) ran the day,
and Poisi Eine took care of the food. Weddings here have a habit of lasting
until sunrise: the couple make their nest in the Rabbit Hole Hotel, and everyone
gathers in the Heart.

If a wedding is still only an idea, write to
[info@vango.ee](mailto:info@vango.ee) with your dates, a rough headcount and
what you would like to eat.
