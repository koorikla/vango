The neighbours have long said that a bear moves through these parts. It is
still hard to believe it happens quite this close.

The trail camera has now settled the argument: a bear really is a visitor here.
Calm, unhurried, going about its business as though the path were entirely its
own.

It is a good reminder that the Wonderland belongs first of all to the forest,
and that we are the guests. Keep food in the tent or the car, take your rubbish
with you, and when you walk into the woods, talk quietly — not out of fear, but
out of manners.

And honestly: this is exactly why people come here.
