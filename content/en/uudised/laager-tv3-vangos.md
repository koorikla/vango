In the autumn of 2021, the Estonian television channel TV3 aired “Laager” —
“The Camp” — a reality series filmed here at Vango.

Over the course of it, nine people of very different ages tried, with the help
of guides, to turn a new page in their lives. What do you do when you no longer
like yourself? When your whole existence feels pointless, when you are wrestling
with depression, addiction, a marriage worn down to routine, or simply a general
weariness with living? How do you find new direction, and the joy of being alive
again?

The first episode went out on Wednesday, 27 October, at half past nine.

Anyone who has been to the Wonderland will recognise a place or two in the
footage — and will also know why people tend to leave here a little different
from how they arrived.
