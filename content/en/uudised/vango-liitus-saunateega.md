In the spring of 2021 the [Sauna Road](https://www.saunatee.ee) opened — a
register gathering more than a hundred Estonian saunas onto a single map. The
Wonderland's saunas are now listed there.

We keep ourselves tucked away in a quiet place, and yet here we are: alongside
the [Romantic Coastal Road](http://www.rannatee.ee/), we are now on the Sauna
Road too.

A sauna is invigorating and calming at the same time. That is a rather good
combination in a life where people are often stressed, worn down by the ordinary
week, and short of ways to recover. Whether you lift your mood by resting or by
working your body, the sauna offers both.

The Water Guardian's Smoke Sauna sits on its own islet in the middle of the
little lake; the Journeyer's Sauna has a hot tub on its terrace. Just ask.
