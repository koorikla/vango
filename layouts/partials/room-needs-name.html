{{- /* Does this room's text need its name put in front of it? Most room texts were written to run straight on from the title, which sits inline immediately before them on the one-pager: "…on eriti müstiline paik", "…asub järve pervel". On a page of their own, under a heading, those read as fragments. Two rooms are not written that way — Imedemaa opens "Endisele metsavahikohale rajatud Imedemaa on eriline paik", a whole sentence — and prepending the name there produces "Imedemaa Endisele metsavahikohale rajatud Imedemaa on…". So the test is the text itself, not the room: a body that opens with a lower-case letter is a continuation and needs its subject back. Both halves of the check matter — `lower` alone would also match a digit or a quotation mark, which begin a sentence perfectly well. Takes a page, returns a boolean. */ -}} {{- $first := substr (trim (.Content | plainify) " \n\t") 0 1 -}} {{- $isCasedLetter := ne (lower $first) (upper $first) -}} {{- return (and $isCasedLetter (eq $first (lower $first))) -}}
